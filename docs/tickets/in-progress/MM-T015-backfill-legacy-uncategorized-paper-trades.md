# Backfill category on legacy uncategorized paper trades

## Problem
The dashboard's per-category P&L (MM-T012) showed an "unmapped" bucket of
-$145.30 across 10 trades, flagged there as "the biggest drag, investigate."
Investigation: it is **not a losing strategy and not an ongoing bug**. Category
tracking on paper picks began 2026-06-23. The five cycles opened before that
(2026-06-09 .. 2026-06-15) carried no category, so the trades that closed them
had nothing to inherit and fell into "unmapped." There are **zero** unmapped
closes after 2026-06-23.

The real damage was to attribution, not P&L: the two biggest early single-name
losers (SPCX -$150.76, ROKU -$58.39) were hiding in "unmapped" instead of
dragging down `single_name`. That made single_name look like the breadwinner
when it is actually the worst bucket.

## Decision
Backfill the 10 legacy trades from `data/briefings.jsonl` (the source of truth
for each pick's category), not by guessing. A closed trade on cycle N closed a
position opened on cycle N-1, so the category is looked up in the briefing dated
N-1 by ticker. All 10 resolved exactly (verified by ticker match).

One-shot, idempotent migration: `scripts/backfill_legacy_categories.py`
(dry-run by default, `--apply` to write). It refuses to write any category it
cannot verify against a briefing, and re-serializes with the app's compact
JSON separators so untouched rows stay byte-identical (diff = only the 10 edits).

## Effect on attribution (current 46-row ledger, $15k sizing amplifies recent trades)
| Category | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| geopolitical | +309.58 | **+331.10** (real winner) |
| commodity | +5.83 | +5.83 |
| unmapped | -145.30 | **gone** |
| macro | -17.98 | -21.24 |
| single_name | -936.89 | **-1100.45** (worst bucket) |

Note: pnl_abs mixes $1k-notional (pre-2026-08-02) and $15k-notional (post) trades,
so the dollar totals overweight recent trades ~15:1. The *direction* is the signal:
single_name is the loser, geopolitical the winner. This kills the earlier
"single_name is the breadwinner" read (an artifact of the hidden legacy losers).
